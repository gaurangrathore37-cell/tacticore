Football Terminology

Common terms used by players, coaches and commentators.

TermSimple meaning
AssistA pass or action credited for setting up a goal.
Clean sheetA match in which a team concedes no goals.
CounterattackA quick attack after winning the ball.
Through ballA pass into space behind defenders.
PressingCoordinated pressure to win back possession.
High lineA defensive line positioned relatively far from its own goal.
Low blockA compact defensive shape closer to the team's own goal.
Overlapping runA teammate runs around the outside of the player with the ball.
Underlapping runA teammate runs inside the player with the ball.
One-twoA pass followed by movement to receive a return pass.
Switch of playMoving the ball from one side to the other.
PossessionControl of the ball.
Set pieceA planned restart, such as a corner or free kick.
Hat-trickWhen one player scores three goals in a match.
TransitionThe change from attack to defence or defence to attack.
Second ballA loose ball available after an initial challenge or aerial duel.
