The foundations

Rules of Football

A simple introduction to the main rules of the game.

Match Basics

  • Each team normally starts with 11 players, including a goalkeeper.
  • A standard match has two 45-minute halves.
  • The referee applies the Laws of the Game.
  • The team scoring more goals wins, unless the match ends in a draw.

Offside

Being in an offside position alone is not an offence. A player may be penalised if they become involved in active play from that position when a teammate plays the ball.

There is no offside offence directly from a goal kick, throw-in or corner kick.

Fouls and Penalties

Illegal challenges can lead to free kicks and cards. A direct-free-kick offence by a defender inside their own penalty area usually results in a penalty kick.

Cards

Yellow card A caution.

Red card The player is sent off and cannot continue playing in that match.

Restarts

  • Kick-off: starts a half or restarts after a goal.
  • Throw-in: when the ball fully crosses the touchline.
  • Goal kick: when the attacking team last touches the ball before it crosses the goal line without a goal.
  • Corner kick: when the defending team last touches the ball before it crosses their goal line without a goal.

Respect and Fair Play

Play fairly, respect opponents and officials, and follow your coach's instructions.
