Know your role

Football Positions

Each position has a role, but players must work together.

🧤 Goalkeeper

Saves shots, protects the goal, communicates with defenders and helps start attacks.

🛡️ Centre-back

Defends the central area, tracks strikers, blocks shots and helps build attacks.

↔️ Full-back

Defends wide areas and can support attacks with runs, crosses and passing options.

⚙️ Defensive Midfielder

Protects the space in front of the defence, intercepts passes and helps control possession.

🔁 Central Midfielder

Connects defence and attack, offers passing angles, scans before receiving and helps keep possession.

🎯 Attacking Midfielder

Creates chances between the opponent's lines and combines with forwards.

💨 Winger

Uses wide spaces to dribble, cross or cut inside, while helping the team defend when needed.

⚽ Striker

Makes attacking runs, links play, presses opponents and tries to score goals.

Midfielder's Checklist

  1. Scan before receiving the ball.
  2. Offer a clear passing angle.
  3. Keep your body open to see the pitch.
  4. Play simply when under pressure.
  5. When possession is lost, help protect central space.
  6. Press when supported rather than chasing the ball blindly.

Remember

Modern positions are flexible. Follow your coach's instructions and communicate with teammates.
