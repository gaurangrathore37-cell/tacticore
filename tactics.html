Football Tactics

Tactics help teams use space, move the ball, create chances and defend together.

Build-up Play

Move the ball from defence into midfield and attack using passing options and good spacing. Do not force risky passes when safer options exist.

Pressing

Apply coordinated pressure to win the ball or force a mistake. Teammates should cover passing options behind the pressing player.

Counterattack

Attack quickly after regaining possession, especially when the opponent has players forward. If the opportunity is not there, keep the ball and rebuild.

Defensive Shape

Protect dangerous central spaces, keep sensible distances between lines, track runners and communicate. Avoid everyone chasing the ball at once.

Width and Depth

Width stretches the opponent from side to side. Depth provides forward and backward passing options.

Switching Play

Move the ball from one side to the other when the opponent crowds one area. This may create space on the far side.

One-Two Pass

Pass to a teammate and immediately move into space to receive the return pass.

Midfielder's Quick Guide

  1. Scan before receiving.
  2. Offer a passing angle.
  3. Receive with an open body position.
  4. Play simply under pressure.
  5. Recover centrally when possession is lost.
  6. Press when teammates can support you.
